Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream is already a big game, but what if you want to make it even bigger? That's where modding comes in!

In Living the Dream, we can take advantage of the very modular structure of the game to create mods that can change almost anything in the game, from simple things like changing the text to more complex things like changing gameplay parameters or even adding new items.

## Getting Started
To begin your journey of creating mods, you'll first need to extract the needed files from your game. You can do this by following our [Dumping RomFS Guide](../../guides/dumping-romfs).
